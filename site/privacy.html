Brightglow Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 25, 2026

THIS POLICY EXPLAINS WHAT INFORMATION BRIGHTGLOW COLLECTS, HOW WE USE AND SHARE IT, AND THE CHOICES YOU HAVE. WE DO NOT SELL YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION, AND WE DO NOT USE THIRD-PARTY ADVERTISING OR CROSS-APP TRACKING.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy describes how Brightglow Technologies LLC, a California limited liability company ("Brightglow," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Brightglow mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Platform"). It is incorporated by reference into the Brightglow Terms of Service. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Terms.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Account information. When you sign in, we collect your email address. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email (and, if you choose, your name) from that provider; we do not receive your password. We verify identity by sending a one-time code to your email.
  • Project information. Photos, annotations, descriptions, and other details you submit to generate an estimate or a request.
  • Messages. The content of messages you exchange with matched businesses or consumers through the Platform.
  • Business and subscription information. If you use the Platform as a service professional, your business name, contact details, service categories, and any license numbers or work photos you provide. Payment for a business subscription is processed by Stripe; Brightglow does not receive, store, or have access to your full card number.

Information collected automatically:

  • Approximate location. With your permission, we access your device location (only while you are using the app, at approximately city-level accuracy) to find nearby professionals and produce location-appropriate estimates. You can grant or revoke this permission in your device settings; if you decline, you can enter a location manually.
  • Device and usage information. Basic technical data needed to operate the Platform, such as app version, device type, and general log information. We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Information from third parties: We obtain business listing details from mapping and business-data providers, and contractor license status from public licensing sources (for example, the California Contractors State License Board). This information may be inaccurate or out of date, as described in the Terms.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate the Platform: create and secure your account, generate AI-powered cost estimates, and match consumers with service professionals;
  • To enable messaging and deliver Leads and notifications by in-app message, email, or push notification;
  • To process business subscription payments through Stripe;
  • To improve estimation accuracy and the quality of matching, including through calibration and feedback on prior estimates;
  • To provide support, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our Terms, and comply with legal obligations.

4. AI Processing of Photos and Descriptions

To generate estimates, the photos and descriptions you submit are processed by automated systems, which may include third-party service providers that provide AI or cloud computing on our behalf under contractual confidentiality and security obligations. These providers process your content only to perform services for Brightglow and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. Estimates are informational only, as described in Section 3 of the Terms.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only as follows:

  • With other users, to enable a match. When you are matched, limited request details and consent-gated contact information are shared between the consumer and the matched business so you can communicate. Do not share more than you are comfortable disclosing.
  • With service providers ("subprocessors") that operate the Platform on our behalf — for example, Supabase (authentication and database hosting), Stripe (payments), email delivery providers (transactional and Lead-notification email), mapping and business-data providers, and AI/cloud processing providers. They may access information only to perform services for us.
  • For legal reasons — to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, or protect the rights, safety, and property of Brightglow, our users, or the public.
  • In a business transfer — in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Platform, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When you delete your account, we purge your associated data as described in Section 7. Some records may be retained where required by law or for legitimate business purposes such as fraud prevention and financial recordkeeping.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • Delete your account. You can permanently delete your account and its data — including your requests, messages, and photos — at any time from within the app (account settings → "Delete account"). This action cannot be undone.
  • Location. You can revoke location access at any time in your device settings.
  • Communications. You can manage push and email notification preferences in settings. We send transactional messages (such as sign-in codes, Lead notifications, and billing notices) that are necessary to provide the service.
  • Access and correction. You can review and update your account information in the app, or contact us for assistance.

8. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) gives you the right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose; to request access to and deletion of your personal information; to correct inaccurate personal information; and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. In the preceding twelve months, we collect the categories of information described in Section 2 (identifiers such as email; approximate geolocation; photos and other content you submit; commercial and subscription information; and internet/device activity), use them for the purposes in Section 3, and disclose them to the recipients in Section 5.

We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under California law. To exercise your rights, use the in-app controls in Section 7 or email hello@brightglow.co; we will verify your request by reference to the email address associated with your account. You may use an authorized agent where permitted by law.

"Shine the Light" (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.83). We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes.

9. Do Not Track

The Platform does not track users across third-party websites or apps and does not respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals because we do not perform such tracking in the first place.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's Privacy

The Platform is intended for users who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Data Location

Brightglow operates in the United States, and your information is processed and stored in the United States. If you access the Platform from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be processed here.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time by posting a revised version with a new "Last Updated" date and, where required by law or where changes are material, providing additional notice. Continued use of the Platform after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.
