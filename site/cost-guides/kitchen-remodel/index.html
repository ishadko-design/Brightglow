How much does a kitchen remodel cost?

New cabinets, counters, and appliances — or a whole new layout — what it costs, and what moves the price:

$14,500–$42,000

National average $27,000

Bay Area: $45,000–$120,000 typical — roughly 35–45% above the national average.

Why quotes run $14,500 to $130,000+

Cabinets
Cabinets are 30–40% of the budget — the single biggest line item. Stock is cheapest; semi-custom and custom climb fast.
Layout changes
Moving the sink or stove means new plumbing, gas, and electrical — $2,000–$5,000 for that work alone. Keeping the footprint saves thousands and weeks.
Counters & appliances
Countertops run $1,900–$4,400; appliances $3,000–$20,000. Stone and pro-grade appliances are where budgets go to die.
Surprises
About 25% of kitchen renovations uncover outdated wiring, corroded pipes, or water damage behind the walls. Hold 15–20% in reserve.
Scope

Refresh or gut

Cosmetic refresh
$3,500–$20,000

Paint or reface cabinets, new hardware, backsplash, a new sink and faucet. Same layout, same boxes.

Full gut renovation
$65,000–$130,000+

Walls move, plumbing and electrical reroute, everything new. The forever-kitchen price.

Bottom line: if the layout works, refresh it. A gut job only pays when the kitchen's bones are wrong — and it costs several times the refresh.

Common questions

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Six to twelve weeks for a full remodel, depending on complexity. A cosmetic refresh can wrap in a week or two.

What costs the most in a kitchen remodel?

Cabinets — 30–40% of the total. Then countertops and appliances. Pick the cabinets first; everything else follows the budget they leave.

Should I move my sink or stove?

Only if the layout truly doesn't work. Relocating plumbing and gas runs $2,000–$5,000 before you buy a single cabinet.

Is a kitchen remodel worth it?

Minor remodels return most of their cost at resale — about $19,200 back on a $20,000 job. Major overhauls return roughly half, so remodel for yourself, not the next buyer.
