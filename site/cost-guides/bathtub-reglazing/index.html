How much does bathtub reglazing cost?

Reglazing — also called refinishing or resurfacing — means spraying a new bonded coating on the tub you already have. What it costs, and what moves the price:

Why quotes run $250 to $1,000+

Material
Porcelain, cast iron, and enamel steel: $350–$600. Fiberglass: $300–$1,000. Antique and clawfoot tubs: $500–$1,400.
Size
A tub-shower combo with surround walls runs $600–$1,000. The extra wall area is what you're paying for.
Condition
A few chips is a basic job. Deep cracks, rust, or stripping a failed older coating adds labor. The prep matters more than the coating — that's what decides how long it lasts.
Color & location
White costs the least. A custom color adds to the price. Most of the bill is labor, so prices move with local labor rates. On this job, San Francisco runs about in line with the national average — not the big-city premium people expect.

DIY vs. pro

DIY kit
$30–$150

Brush- or roll-on epoxy. It tends to show every stroke and lasts 2–5 years at best. You do the stripping, etching, and ventilating yourself. If it fails, stripping it costs about $200 more before a pro will take the job.

Professional
$350–$650

A sprayed-on industrial coating that lasts 10–15 years with gentle cleaning. The work takes 3–5 hours, with ventilation running. Most pros put a warranty on the finish in writing — ask for it.

Bottom line: a kit can make sense for a guest bath that doesn't get much use. In a daily-use bathroom, the pro job costs about four kits and lasts five times as long. If the tub is cracked through, flexes, or leaks, skip reglazing — a coating is cosmetic, not structural.

Common questions

How long does a reglazed bathtub last?

A professionally reglazed tub typically lasts 10 to 15 years with gentle, non-abrasive cleaning. DIY kits usually last 2 to 5 years at best, and often less in a daily-use bathroom.

How long does bathtub reglazing take?

About 3 to 5 hours of work. After that, wait 24 to 48 hours before running water on it, so the coating can cure.

Is reglazing the same as refinishing or resurfacing?

Yes — reglazing, refinishing, and resurfacing are three names for the same job: strip and repair the old surface, then spray on a new bonded coating. Ask for the prep steps in writing, whatever it's called.

When should I replace the tub instead of reglazing it?

Replace when the tub is cracked through, flexes or leaks, has structural rust, or when you want to change its size or layout anyway. Reglazing is a new surface, not a structural repair.
