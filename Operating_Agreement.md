# OPERATING AGREEMENT OF BRIGHTGLOW TECHNOLOGIES LLC

**A California Single-Member Limited Liability Company**

This Operating Agreement (the "Agreement") of **Brightglow Technologies LLC** (the "Company"), a limited liability company organized under the laws of the State of California, is entered into and effective as of **July 23, 2026** by **Ihor Shadko** (the "Member").

---

## 1. Formation
1.1 **Formation.** The Company was formed by filing Articles of Organization with the California Secretary of State on **July 23, 2026**, under Entity No. **B20260337056**. The Company is governed by the California Revised Uniform Limited Liability Company Act (the "Act") and this Agreement. If there is any conflict, this Agreement controls to the extent permitted by law.

1.2 **Name.** The business of the Company shall be conducted under the name **Brightglow Technologies LLC**, or such other name as the Member may designate.

1.3 **Principal Office.** The principal office of the Company is **332 Peoria Street, Daly City, CA 94014**, or such other place as the Member may determine.

1.4 **Registered Agent.** The Company's agent for service of process in California is **Ihor Shadko**, located at **332 Peoria Street, Daly City, CA 94014**.

1.5 **Term.** The Company began on its formation date and shall continue perpetually unless dissolved under this Agreement or by law.

## 2. Purpose
The Company is organized for the purpose of engaging in **the operation of the Brightglow platform connecting consumers with local businesses/contractors, and any other lawful business** for which a limited liability company may be organized under California law.

## 3. Member
3.1 **Sole Member.** The Member owns **100%** of the membership interests in the Company.

| Member | Ownership | Capital Contribution |

3.2 **Limited Liability.** The Member shall not be personally liable for any debts, obligations, or liabilities of the Company beyond the Member's capital contribution, except as required by law. The Company is a legal entity separate and distinct from the Member.

## 4. Capital Contributions
4.1 **Initial Contribution.** The Member has contributed **$2,000** in cash and/or property to the Company.

4.2 **Additional Contributions.** The Member is not obligated to make additional contributions but may do so at the Member's discretion.

## 5. Distributions and Allocations
5.1 **Distributions.** The Member may cause the Company to distribute available cash or property to the Member at such times and amounts as the Member determines, provided the Company remains able to pay its debts as they come due.

5.2 **Profits and Losses.** As a single-member LLC, all profits and losses are allocated 100% to the Member.

5.3 **Tax Treatment.** The Company is by default a disregarded entity for federal income tax purposes; its income and expenses are reported on the Member's individual return, unless the Member elects otherwise (e.g., S-corporation or C-corporation election).

## 6. Management
6.1 **Member-Managed.** The Company is managed by its Member, who has full and exclusive authority to manage and control the business, including entering into contracts, opening accounts, hiring, and all other decisions.

6.2 **Authority to Bind.** The Member is authorized to sign on behalf of the Company. Contracts and obligations shall be executed in the Company's name in the form: "Brightglow Technologies LLC, by Ihor Shadko, Member."

6.3 **Officers.** The Member may appoint officers or agents and delegate duties as the Member sees fit.

## 7. Books, Records, and Accounts
7.1 **Separate Finances.** The Company shall maintain its own bank account(s) and financial records, separate from the personal accounts and property of the Member. Company funds shall not be commingled with personal funds.

7.2 **Records.** The Company shall keep accurate books and records at its principal office.

7.3 **Fiscal Year.** The Company's fiscal year ends on **December 31**.

## 8. Transfer of Interest
The Member may sell, assign, or transfer all or part of the membership interest at the Member's discretion. Upon admission of an additional member, this Agreement shall be amended or restated to reflect a multi-member structure.

## 9. Dissolution
9.1 The Company shall dissolve upon the written decision of the Member, or as required by law.

9.2 Upon dissolution, the Company's assets shall be applied first to creditors, then to the Member, and the Company shall file a Certificate of Cancellation with the California Secretary of State.

## 10. Indemnification
The Company shall indemnify the Member and any officers or agents against liabilities incurred in the course of acting on behalf of the Company, to the fullest extent permitted by the Act, except for acts of fraud, gross negligence, or willful misconduct.

## 11. General Provisions
11.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.

11.2 **Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only in writing signed by the Member.

11.3 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid, the remaining provisions remain in effect.

11.4 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement of the Member concerning the Company.
